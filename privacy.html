How it works.

  • Your work is saved on your Mac.
  • When you ask Pip for help, what that job needs goes to the AI you've chosen, and the answer comes back to your Mac.
  • With the Pip subscription, that AI runs on EU servers and isn't trained on your work.
  • With your own Claude plan or API key, that provider's terms apply.
  • Set Pip's AI to a model on your Mac, and what you ask stays on it. Web searches and pictures, if you use them, still go out.
