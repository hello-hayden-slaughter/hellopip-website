Download Pip for Mac

Pick the one for your Mac. To check which you have, open the Apple menu, then About This Mac: a line called Chip means Apple silicon, and a Processor line that says Intel means Intel.

Apple silicon Mac Intel Mac

It runs on macOS 13 or later. It's about 300 MB.

  1. 1

    Open the download. It's called Pip, in your Downloads folder.

  2. 2

    Drag Pip into Applications. Then open it from there.

  3. 3

    Sign in with your Apple ID. And you're in.
