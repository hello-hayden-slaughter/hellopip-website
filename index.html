the idea

Your workspace isn't a staging area. It's the record.

Send a note out to Slack or Notion and it doesn't leave. It stays here, as the version you both keep working on, and it's still findable in six months.

your notes

Write together in the same document.

Ask Pip for a section and he writes it straight into the note, rather than handing you a wall of text in the chat to copy out yourself. Open the side chat and he edits whatever you've got in front of you, in place.

Reading along as you write, he'll drop a question in the margin to sharpen the thinking, without ever taking it over.

"Write in the note, not about the note."

on your machine

Yours, and under your control.

Pip lives on your computer. Your notes, your context and your history are saved on your own hardware, not on a server of ours.

01 / local-first

Stays on your machine.

Everything Pip knows is stored on your disk. Nothing leaves it unless you connect a service or send something out yourself.

02 / any model

Pick a model, any model.

Connect one key through OpenRouter, or run a local model that never leaves your Mac. If the model you've picked can't handle a particular turn, Pip tells you and offers one of your others that can, instead of switching behind your back.

03 / no surprises

Nothing changes silently.

When Pip learns something about how you work, he shows you the change and waits for you to approve it. He never edits himself behind your back.

questions

Things people ask.

Is Pip free?
Yes, while it's in beta. When I land on a pricing model you'll hear about it before anything changes, and there'll always be a free tier that runs entirely on your own machine.
Does it work on Windows or Linux?
Mac only for now. Windows and Linux are on the list, but I want Mac to be genuinely good before I split my attention.
What's an LLM, and does it matter which one?
It's the part that does the actual thinking. Claude, GPT and Gemini are the well known ones. Pip is the layer that connects one of them to your workspace and your tools. Different models are good at different things, and some run entirely on your machine, so Pip lets you pick one and switch whenever you want.
Who's building this?
Just me. I'm Hayden, based in Brighton. I got tired of AI tools that confidently wrote things I hadn't asked for, so I started building the one I actually wanted.
