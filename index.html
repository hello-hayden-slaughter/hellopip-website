Why I built Pip

“

I used Claude for real work. Then I'd go looking for something I'd made last week and find it buried in a conversation. I couldn't write my own notes into it, it didn't sit with the rest of my work, and every change went through a chat box. So I built Pip. The work sits in the middle, where I can see it. The AI comes to it.”
